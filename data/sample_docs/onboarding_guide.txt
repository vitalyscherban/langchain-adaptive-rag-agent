Acme Cloud Platform - Onboarding Guide
======================================

Step 1: Create an organization
Sign up at acme.cloud/signup and create an organization. An organization is
the top-level container for all your projects, billing, and team members.
You will be prompted to verify your email address before proceeding.

Step 2: Create your first project
Projects group related resources (compute instances, storage buckets,
databases) under a shared quota and IAM policy. We recommend creating
separate projects for development, staging, and production environments to
keep resource limits and access controls isolated.

Step 3: Invite team members
From the organization settings page, invite teammates by email and assign
them a role: Owner, Admin, Developer, or Viewer. Owners have full control
including billing; Developers can create and modify resources but cannot
change billing or delete the organization; Viewers have read-only access.

Step 4: Configure your first resource
Use the console, CLI (`acme` command), or Terraform provider to provision
your first compute instance or storage bucket. New organizations receive
$100 in free trial credit, valid for 30 days, to explore the platform before
adding a payment method.

Step 5: Set up monitoring and alerts
Enable the built-in monitoring dashboard to track resource usage, and
configure alert thresholds (e.g. CPU > 80% for 10 minutes) to be notified via
email or webhook before issues affect production traffic.

Support channels
Community support is available via the public forum. Paid plans include
email support with a 24-hour response SLA, and Enterprise plans include a
dedicated Slack channel with a 1-hour response SLA during business hours.
