Acme Cloud Platform - Billing FAQ
==================================

How does billing work?
Acme Cloud bills customers monthly in arrears based on metered usage across
compute, storage, and network egress. Invoices are generated on the first day
of each month and are due within 15 days (Net 15).

What payment methods are supported?
We support credit card, ACH bank transfer, and wire transfer for enterprise
accounts. Credit card is the default for self-serve accounts under $5,000/month
in spend. ACH and wire transfer require a signed order form.

Can I get a refund?
Refunds are issued at Acme's discretion for verified billing errors, such as
duplicate charges or metering bugs. Refund requests must be submitted within
60 days of the invoice date through the billing support portal.

What happens if a payment fails?
If a payment fails, we retry automatically after 3 days and again after 7
days. After the second failed retry, the account is moved to a "past due"
state and non-essential resources (e.g. staging environments) may be
suspended. Production resources are not suspended until 30 days past due.

How do I change my billing plan?
Billing plan changes take effect at the start of the next billing cycle.
Downgrades that reduce reserved capacity may require confirming that current
usage fits within the new plan's limits before the change is applied.

Do you offer volume discounts?
Yes. Customers with sustained monthly spend above $10,000 are eligible for
volume discounts of 5-15%, negotiated through their account manager. Discounts
are not automatically applied and must be requested.
